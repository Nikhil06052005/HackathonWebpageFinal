Secrets of the Demon Slayers

What is SGU Hackathon-2025?
SGU Hackathon-2025 is a 12-hour battle where only the strongest Demon Slayers (developers) will survive! It is an Open Innovation Hackathon, meaning you can forge your own Nichirin Blade (project) in any domain.
When and where will the hackathon take place?
📅 Date: 12 April 2025
⏳ Duration: 12 Hours of intense battle
📍 Demon Slayer HQ: Architecture-Building, SDGI Campus, Ghaziabad
When and how can I register?
🔥 Registration Opens: 21st March 2025
❌ Registration Closes: 5th April 2025
🏹 Registration is FREE!
Who can participate?
Only the bravest warriors! Whether you’re a 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th-year student from B.Tech or BCA, your sword (code) is your strength!
What is the team size limit?
🗡️ Minimum: Solo Demon Slayer (1 person)
⚔️ Maximum: A full Hashira Squad (4 members)
Will there be free food during the hackathon?
Yes! Just like a Hashira gets special meals after battle, all warriors will get free food and refreshments to stay strong!
What are the prizes for the winners?
🏆 Total Prize Pool: ₹1,00,000
🥇 1st Prize: ₹XX,XXX – Sun Breathing Champion
🥈 2nd Prize: ₹XX,XXX – Moon Breathing Master
How will projects be judged?
🔥 Innovation & Creativity
⚔️ Technical Implementation
🌍 Feasibility & Scalability
Is there any specific technology or theme?
No! This is an Open Innovation Hackathon, meaning you can use any technology and battle in any domain!
Can I submit a pre-built project?
No, a true Demon Slayer fights in real-time! All projects must be built during the hackathon.
